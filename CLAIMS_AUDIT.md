# Claims audit (Stage 1 and 1b re-derivation, 2026-09-08)

Every quantitative or causal claim the paper makes, with the number it rested on, the
re-derived number, and a verdict. Tables with the underlying numbers are generated by
`scripts/claims_audit.py` into `CLAIMS_AUDIT_TABLES.md` and `outputs/claims_audit.json`,
comparing the superseded files in `outputs/_superseded_20260908/` with the Stage 1 and
1b outputs. Every re-derived checkpoint descends from the July 4 baseline
(`outputs/checkpoint_manifest.json`), the retrain reference is the clean retain-split
retrain (five seeds; per-size clean retrains for the ablations), and all privacy numbers
come from one CPU attacker pass per forget set. Success rule: method advantage at or
below the retrain nested-CI upper bound, now 0.182. "LR" is the reference-model
likelihood-ratio audit (negative ELBO under clean retrain seed 42 minus negative ELBO
under the target; near 1 means the forget cells are still fitted better than a
never-trained model fits them, near 0 means worse).

HOLDS = same conclusion, numbers within noise. CHANGES = conclusion survives but the
statement, number, or mechanism must be rewritten. FAILS = not supported. NEW = a result
the paper did not have.
