Fishing for Privacy, reviewed against the code and the checkpoints

Rounds 1 to 4 checked that the paper's numbers trace to JSON files. This round checked the JSON files against the scripts that wrote them and the checkpoints they describe. The paper, the 40 notebooks, everything under src/ and scripts/, the result JSONs, and the weights on disk were all read.

Not submittable as it stands.

Three defects in the evidence base sit underneath the text and invalidate most of the headline numbers. None is visible from the paper or the JSONs, which is why four rounds passed them. Each is established from files on disk, and each has a bounded repair.

B1 The retrain reference trained on 82 to 88 percent of its own matched negatives. Retrain floor, success threshold, refit control, cluster-12 reference, PBMC confound fractions, every "not separable from retrain" claim. Five clean seeds trained today, 90 s each. Clean floor is 0.049, not 0.156.
B2 The baseline checkpoint was regenerated on July 4, after most method checkpoints were derived from the old one. Table 2 mixes two parents; the SSD, SCRUB, and contrastive utility columns describe checkpoints that no longer exist. Re-derive four method families from one baseline; re-score in one pass.
B3 Fisher scrubbing and extra-gradient, as implemented, are not the methods the paper describes. Every Fisher and extra-gradient number; the "posterior collapse" and "unreliable across seeds" narratives; the attack-suite table. Fix two loss functions and one training loop; re-run.

HoldsThe biology-confound measurement on TCGA-BRCA and on PBMC cluster 7 is clean. The Fisher-overlap analysis of the current baseline is internally consistent. The SSD, SCRUB, contrastive, Moon, and DP-SGD privacy rows descend from the current baseline with correct loss functions and stand as reported.

B3Two methods are not what the paper says they are

Fisher scrubbing

Three departures from the text in src/train_fisher_unlearn.py.

  1. The paper says the diagonal Fisher is taken on the forget set. The code computes it on the retain set, in train mode with dropout active, from batch-summed gradients.
  2. The scrub step is clamp(lr * grad / F, -0.001, 0.001) for 100 steps. With damping 0.1 the clamp saturates for most parameters, so the update is a signed ascent of 0.001 per step regardless of the Fisher weighting. The recorded forget loss rises from 283 to 218,000 during scrubbing and the post-scrub AUC is exactly 0.5. That is a destroyed model, not a scrubbed one.
  3. The retain fine-tune that follows averages reconstruction error over genes while summing KL over latent dimensions. Training sums reconstruction over the 2,000 genes. The fine-tune therefore weights KL 2,000 times more heavily than training did, which forces the posterior toward the prior. The "posterior collapse" reported for Fisher (KL 10.55 to 0.007, marker r 0.628, ELBO 490) is the signature of this objective, not of Fisher overlap. Every other method fine-tunes with vae_loss and keeps KL near 10.

Every Fisher-scrubbing number inherits this (PBMC 0.808 / 0.615, TCGA 0.500 under both matchings, Tabula Muris 0.946, cluster-12 marker r 0.21, the conditional-VAE scrub, the scattered-set table). The Fisher-overlap analysis in Section 7 uses a separate per-sample estimator and is not affected, but the paper's link from that analysis to "this is why Fisher scrubbing collapses the posterior" is not supported by the method as run.

Extra-gradient co-training

Four departures in src/train_unlearn_extragradient.py.

  1. There is no extragradient step. The critic update is one backward pass and a plain SGD step at 1e-5; the Adam critic optimizer is constructed and never stepped.
  2. The critics are trained to separate forget cells from retain cells, which is cluster identity, not membership.
  3. The utility term has the same mean-over-genes reconstruction as the Fisher fine-tune, so its KL is over-weighted 2,000 times, and it is multiplied by lambda 10. The recorded privacy loss is about 0.8 per step against a utility term of about 110.
  4. An "epoch" is one minibatch and one optimizer step. The paper's "50 epochs" never happened. The shadow-attacker early-stopping rule fires whenever a noisy AUC rises twice in a row, and the ten seeds stopped after 3, 4, 10, 10, 11, 11, 11, 12, 18, and 19 steps.

Attack suite

The threshold and likelihood-ratio attacks decode without the library size, so the "reconstruction" they score is a softmax probability vector against log-normalized counts. That is why Table 3 reports the same reconstruction-threshold advantage for every model.

Reconstruction does carry membership signal, and the sentence "reconstruction quality is preserved, so the membership signal lives in the KL and latent geometry" is reversed. The likelihood-ratio row is one statistic reported twice with opposite sign (baseline AUC 0.08 with retrain as reference, retrain AUC 0.92 with baseline as reference). The k-NN row's advantage of 0.986 comes from AUCs of 0.01 to 0.05, an attack whose membership predictions are exactly inverted.

Repair plan

Stage 1
Evidence base (about one working day of compute and scripting).
  1. Adopt the July 4 baseline as the single parent. Re-derive retain-only fine-tune (5 seeds) and gradient ascent (5 seeds) from it with the existing scripts.
  2. Fix Fisher scrubbing (forget-set per-sample Fisher as the text says, a step size that does not saturate the clamp, vae_loss for the fine-tune) and re-run three seeds. If the posterior still collapses, the narrative stands; if not, it goes.
  3. Fix extra-gradient (implement the extragradient step or rename the method; vae_loss for utility; a fixed step budget with no shadow early stop; an epoch as a pass over the retain set) and re-run ten seeds.
  4. Pass the library size in the two attack files and re-run notebook 15.
  5. Re-score every checkpoint, including the clean retrain, with one CPU attacker, and recompute utility for every current checkpoint in one pass. Regenerate tables and figures through the loaders.
  6. Add a checkpoint manifest with hashes and parents, and stop re-executing training notebooks in audits.
Stage 2
Text. Rewrite Sections 4 to 6 and the appendices from the re-scored JSON, then apply the round-4 reframing (measurement as lead finding, calibration citations, retrain as floor, title).
